This project was made to help visualize a particularly stubborn keyboard from an Underwood 5 typewriter, s/n 2394077-5, discovered in Japan with an unique hiragana-kanji layout. Because the keyboard contained different characters for shifted and unshifted printing, a tool like this helps visualize the typewriter; it may prove useful for additional work for other typewriter enthusiasts or researchers interested in how limitations in the predominantly-Western typewriter influenced different cultures' ease and approaches to typing as a method of data input, and eventually their digitalization with the advent of the personal computer.

In this file, the .json file contains my best-guess/reasoned and researched estimates of what characters are where. The actual typewriter belongs to a good friend of mine, and his writeup is at https://typeri.us/posts/hiragana-underwood.  

A future development for this project -- should I get to it, and certainly after this typewriter gets fully demystified -- would be the addition of a keyboard identifying prediction layer or perhaps an option to add keyboards to your device. Don't count on this happening, though.
